Frequently asked questions

Do I need a TeslaMate server?

Yes, for real data. Tessalytics is a client for your own TeslaMate plus TeslaMateApi installation and is not a standalone service. You can explore the entire app without one by tapping Explore Demo on first launch.

The app can't reach my server.

Confirm your iPhone can reach TeslaMateApi on the same network path the app uses — over VPN if the server is private. Check the scheme and port in the URL, any reverse-proxy authentication, and the TLS certificate. The onboarding screen reports the exact failure it saw.

Should I expose TeslaMateApi to the internet?

No. TeslaMateApi's built-in API_TOKEN does not protect all read-only endpoints. If the service is reachable outside a trusted private network, put authentication in front of every route with a VPN or reverse proxy, and never expose PostgreSQL, MQTT, or Tesla tokens.

Where are my credentials stored?

In the iOS Keychain only. Optional Tesla Owner API tokens use when-unlocked, this-device-only protection, so they never leave the device or migrate through a backup. Details are in the privacy policy.

Why does my status say stale or asleep?

Tessalytics labels data honestly rather than showing an old snapshot as current. Asleep means the vehicle is sleeping and TeslaMate is not receiving updates; stale means the last refresh failed and you're seeing cached data. Tessalytics never wakes a sleeping vehicle to refresh.

Are battery health and forecasts accurate?

They are estimates computed on your device from your own logged history — not diagnostics, guarantees, or safety guidance. Accuracy improves as TeslaMate accumulates more data.

How do vehicle commands work?

Commands require the optional Owner API token pair, which you generate outside Tessalytics. Every command needs an in-app confirmation plus Face ID or your device passcode. Nothing runs in the background, and demo mode never enables commands.
