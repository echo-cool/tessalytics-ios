Tessalytics

Understand every drive.

  • Your server, your data. A native iPhone client for your own self-hosted TeslaMate and TeslaMateApi installation. No developer cloud sits in between.
  • Status at a glance. Battery, range, charging, location, climate, security, tire pressure, and software — with honest live, stale, asleep, and offline labels.
  • History that works offline. Synchronized drives and charging sessions with route maps, speed and power charts, charging curves, energy, and cost.
  • Analysis beyond a dashboard. Native analytics, battery-health estimates, and on-device forecasts for travel, charging time, cost, and efficiency.
  • Private by design. No accounts, no advertising, no analytics SDK, no tracking. Credentials live in the Keychain; history stays in the app container.
