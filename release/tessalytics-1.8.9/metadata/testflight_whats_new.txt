Tessalytics 1.8.9 — 简体中文, and the share crash fixed.

简体中文

The app now speaks Simplified Chinese. Settings → 语言 (Language) offers System,
English and 简体中文, and the change lands immediately — no relaunch.

It is deliberately separate from iOS's own per-app language setting, which is
several screens deep in Settings and not where anyone looks. Dates, distances,
currency and number formatting keep following your region, because "what language
do you read" and "what units do you think in" are different questions.

Traditional Chinese is not claimed. It is a different script, and showing it
simplified characters would be worse than showing it English.

If you find an English string on a Chinese screen, tell me which one — some text
is assembled at runtime from numbers and units and has not been converted yet.

THE SHARE BUTTON NO LONGER CRASHES

Reported from a device, with a crash log that named the cause exactly. The poster
was being built inside the share action rather than while the view was being
drawn, and SwiftUI's environment is only valid during a draw — so on pages whose
content reads it, tapping share killed the app. Please try share on every page it
appears on.

AND IT IS FASTER

The home screen fetched its two map images one after the other, each a round trip
to Apple's tile servers. They now run at the same time, and neither can hang: if a
tile server is slow, the poster arrives with a plain panel where the map would be
rather than not arriving at all.

FIXED FROM A DIAGNOSTICS EXPORT

Three problems, all found by reading a real export from a real drive:

- The file was not valid JSON. Redacted coordinates were written as a bare token,
  which broke every recorded event body in it. The export exists to be read by
  something.
- Long numbers were being redacted as if they were VINs. Seventeen digits look
  exactly like a VIN to a pattern that only counts characters, so timings came out
  as "0.[REDACTED]".
- Recording live events pushed everything else out of the log. An export arrived
  holding 394 stream readings, six requests, and not a single state change or
  failure — which are the entries you turn recording on to find. Live events now
  have their own share of the log and stop evicting the diagnosis.
