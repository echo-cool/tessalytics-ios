Tessalytics 1.9.3 — the last five Grafana dashboards, and a picker that ignored you.

THE VARIANT PICKER

On Vehicle rating, "Not set" could be tapped but never took: it always snapped
back to whatever the trim badge suggested. A cleared variant was stored as
nothing at all, and "nothing" already meant "you have not chosen yet" — which is
exactly what licenses the guess. Two more were found beside it: the picker saved
nothing at all for a car this iPhone had never written to its store, and a stored
variant the model does not offer left the control with no row to mark.

TESLAMATE'S DASHBOARDS ARE NOW ALL HERE

Five of TeslaMate's Grafana dashboards had no answer in the app. They do now.

- Efficiency (new): consumption against outside temperature, weighted by distance
  so a short cold trip does not outweigh a long run; consumption by month; and
  consumption against trip length.
- Mileage (new): the odometer curve, distance and charging by month, and where
  the period actually went — driving, charging, or standing still.
- Standby drain, on the Battery page: one point per parked stretch of six hours
  or more, against a rule at your typical daily loss. This is the vampire-drain
  panel, and it is the one battery figure you can act on.

The battery page's metric grid was two rows of three with only four filled. It
carries six now — full cycles and standby drain join the four that were there —
and so do the Overview, Drive, Charge and Mileage grids.

LESS TEXT, EVERYWHERE

Roughly ninety strings are shorter or gone, and about forty on-screen elements
went with them: legends that named a single series already named in the card
title, "Date" written under an axis whose ticks read "Aug 16", subtitles that
repeated their own heading. All six languages moved in step.

WHAT TO TEST

Vehicle rating (tap the car's name on the home screen): every variant, including
"Not set", should stick — and stay stuck after leaving and coming back.

Analysis → Efficiency and Mileage, across several reporting periods. The
temperature bands need drives in genuinely different weather to say anything, so
a car with only a warm month of history will show one band and no cold penalty;
that is correct, not broken.

Analysis → Battery, scrolled to the bottom, for standby drain. If your figure
looks high, Sentry is the usual reason — I would like to hear what you see either
way, and whether the number matches what you would have guessed.

And tell me if any screen now reads as too terse. Cutting text is easy to
overdo, and a label I found redundant may be one you were relying on.
