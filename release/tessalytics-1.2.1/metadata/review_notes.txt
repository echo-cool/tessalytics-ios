NO SERVER, TESLA ACCOUNT, OR CREDENTIALS ARE NEEDED TO REVIEW THIS APP.
PLEASE USE THE BUILT-IN DEMO MODE.

Tessalytics is an unofficial companion app for TeslaMate, an open-source
vehicle logger that users host themselves. It is not a hosted service and has
no account system, so there is nothing to sign up for or sign in to. Normally
the app reads from a server the user already operates — but it ships with a
complete on-device demo precisely so it can be evaluated without one.

HOW TO OPEN DEMO MODE

On the first onboarding screen, tap "Explore Demo". That is the only step.

Tessalytics then generates about two months of sample vehicle data directly on
the device: drives with route maps, charging sessions, analytics, battery-health
trends, software history, and forecasts. Demo mode:

- requires no TeslaMate server, no Tesla account, and no credentials
- works entirely offline (Airplane Mode is fine)
- is clearly labeled as demo data in the UI
- exercises every screen in the app
- never enables vehicle commands
- remains available after relaunch

SUGGESTED WALKTHROUGH

1. Status — vehicle dashboard: battery, range, charging, location, climate,
   security, tire pressure, and honest live/stale/asleep/offline labels.
2. Activity — drive and charging history. Tap any row for route maps, speed and
   power charts, charging curves, energy, and cost.
3. Analysis — distance, energy, cost and efficiency analytics, battery capacity
   and health estimates, and on-device forecasts.
4. Settings — privacy details, software history, notification options, the
   unofficial-project disclaimer, and the optional direct connection.

To exit: Settings > Leave demo, or Settings > Connect a real server.

NOTES ON SCOPE AND PRIVACY

No in-app purchases, subscriptions, account registration, advertising,
analytics SDK, or tracking. Nothing is collected by the developer; there is no
developer-operated backend. Cached history stays in the app container and any
credentials a real user enters go to the Keychain.

The optional Owner API connection is not required for any of the above and is
not part of demo mode. It accepts an access/refresh-token pair that advanced
users generate outside Tessalytics; the app never requests a Tesla password.
Every command it enables requires an in-app confirmation followed by Face ID or
the device passcode, and the app never wakes a sleeping vehicle automatically.

Battery health and forecasts are presented throughout as estimates, not
diagnostics, guarantees, or safety guidance.

Tessalytics is an unofficial community tool and is not affiliated with,
endorsed by, or supported by the official TeslaMate project or Tesla, Inc. No
Tesla or TeslaMate logo, artwork, or vehicle render is used.

Screenshots use generated demo data. No real vehicle, location, route, server,
VIN, or credential appears in them.
