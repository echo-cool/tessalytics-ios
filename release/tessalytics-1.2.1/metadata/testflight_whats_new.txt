No TeslaMate server needed to test this build.

Tap "Explore Demo" on the first onboarding screen. Tessalytics generates about two months of sample drives, charging sessions, routes, analytics, battery trends, and forecasts on the device — no Tesla, no account, no network. Demo mode is labeled throughout and never enables vehicle commands. Leave it from Settings > Leave demo, or Settings > Connect a real server; saved server profiles are preserved.

Please check:
• Status — cached values should appear immediately, with the refresh continuing behind them. Pull-to-refresh should never hang on a slow or unreachable server.
• Freshness labels — live, direct live, stale, asleep, offline, unavailable.
• Activity — route-map previews on drive cards, incremental loading, and offline browsing with the network off.
• Analysis — analytics, battery-health estimates, and forecasts.
• Real servers — Bearer, Basic, and VPN-only connections, plus multi-server and multi-vehicle switching.
• Dark mode and large Dynamic Type.

Battery health and forecasts are estimates, not diagnostics.
