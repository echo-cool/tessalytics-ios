Tessalytics 1.4.0 — the app now asks the server, and the numbers come from your
whole history rather than the last page of it.

WHAT TO TEST FIRST

1. Open the app cold. It used to show whatever was cached until you pulled to
   refresh, which is why "Latest drive" could be days old. It now fetches on
   every open and on every return from the background. Please confirm the latest
   drive is genuinely your latest drive.

2. Leave it open. Live status refreshes every 30 seconds while the app is on
   screen, and history every 5 minutes. Both stop when you background it, so it
   should not cost battery in your pocket.

3. The top card now carries Battery health: capacity when new, usable now, max
   range new and now, and range lost. Check those against your Grafana
   battery-health dashboard — they come from the same /battery-health figures.

4. Drive stats: logged miles, odometer, and data lost. "Data lost" is measured
   from your first logged drive, not from zero, so mileage from before TeslaMate
   existed is not counted as a gap. Compare the captured percentage with what you
   expect from your logger's downtime.

5. Charging totals: charges, charging cycles, energy added, energy used, charging
   efficiency and total cost — summed over every session, not the newest page.
   The first run pages your whole history (about eight requests for 800 drives),
   then only tops up. Totals should match Grafana.

6. Battery > Battery capacity by mileage. Per-charge points with a semi-monthly
   median through them, modelled from the rated range and charge level at the end
   of each charge. Also Projected range over time.

7. Analysis > Charge > Charging by location: a map with bubbles sized by the
   energy delivered at each site, plus a ranked list.

ALSO CHANGED

- Charging history leads with energy added and average power; a price appears
  only where one is configured.
- Energy totals switch to MWh once kWh stops being readable.
- Charging cycles means equivalent full charges, not a count of plug-ins.

KNOWN GAPS

This build does not talk to Tessalytics-API yet — the new backend that serves
the Grafana panels the TeslaMate REST API cannot reach. Everything above still
comes from TeslaMateApi. Wiring the app to the new backend is the next step.

App Store screenshots have not been re-shot, and no iPad set exists yet. Both are
needed before a store submission; neither blocks TestFlight.
