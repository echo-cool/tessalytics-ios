Tessalytics 1.7.1 — live mode actually goes live, and the map draws the drive.

WHAT TO TEST FIRST

Mount the phone and drive. 1.7.0 opened an event stream, reported itself LIVE, and
then discarded every reading it received: the stream decoded an envelope the
backend does not send, so each event failed to parse and was dropped in silence.
Everything on screen still came from the poll behind it. The badge was telling the
truth about the connection and nothing about the data.

1. The speed on screen should now move continuously rather than in steps. The
   server pushes a reading roughly every 0.4 seconds while driving, which is the
   rate TeslaMate publishes at, and that is what the app now renders.

2. The hero card reads "updated at 20:25:18" instead of "updated just now". This
   is there so a delay can be measured rather than guessed: watch the seconds
   while driving, and the clock time should track your own. If it stalls, the
   number beside it is stale and now says so.

3. The map draws the route of the drive in progress, not a pin. Open the app
   halfway through a journey and the road behind the car should already be there —
   the part before the app was listening is fetched from the server and joined to
   the live readings. The camera frames the route and steps its zoom as the drive
   grows, rather than rescaling on every reading.

ALSO FIXED

- Reconnecting no longer ages its backoff across working connections. Six tunnels
  on one drive used to walk the retry delay up to twenty seconds; a connection
  that stayed up now starts again at one second.
- The stream is opened when a vehicle is selected and when a server is first
  saved, not only when the app returns to the foreground. Those sessions silently
  ran on the thirty-second poll with the LIVE badge lit.
- Streamed readings are no longer written to the on-disk cache individually. Two
  or three saves a second on the main thread cost the interface the responsiveness
  the stream exists to provide; the cache is written every few seconds and
  immediately whenever the state changes.
- The live charts draw a thinned series. Fifteen minutes of readings is over two
  thousand marks in a chart a few hundred points wide, redrawn on every reading.
- The stream now asks for the same units as the poll. Both paths were relying on
  the server's default agreeing with them, so a scale change on the server would
  have shown a speed in the wrong unit depending on which route delivered it.

KNOWN GAPS

The route drawn on the hero map needs a backend with `/v1/vehicles/{id}/track`.
Without it the map falls back to the readings taken since the app opened.

App Store screenshots still do not cover live mode.
