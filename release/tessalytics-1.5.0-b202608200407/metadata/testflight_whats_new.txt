Tessalytics 1.5.0 (202608200407) — chart correctness, and the sync bug behind
the "Offline" banner.

WHAT TO TEST FIRST

1. Open any drive and look at the Speed chart against the "Maximum speed" card
   above it. They agree now. They did not before: Swift Charts stacks an area
   mark by default, which for a dense series sums neighbouring samples — a drive
   that peaked at 33 mph drew spikes to 155 on an axis running to 200. The same
   fault hit every charge chart. A Supercharger session should now show one
   taper from its peak, matching its own "Peak power" figure.

2. Scroll to Elevation and Outside temperature on a drive. These no longer
   anchor to zero. An elevation of 15-20 m and a temperature that moves half a
   degree were both flat lines against a zero baseline; they are framed to their
   own range now, so the variation is visible. Speed, power and energy still keep
   zero, because zero means something there.

3. Battery > Battery capacity by mileage. The x-axis starts where your data
   starts. If the car was logged from 15,000 miles, the chart no longer spends
   the first two thirds of the plot on mileage that has no readings.

4. Charge history addresses. One line each: "Tesla Supercharger, San Jose"
   rather than the full OpenStreetMap string down to the county and postcode.

5. The home screen after a cold start. Check there is no "Offline — showing
   synchronized data" banner and no "Stale" badge while the server is reachable.
   A cursor bug stopped drive paging after the first 100 records, and that
   failure aborted the run before charges were fetched at all — which is why the
   card said "No charges synced" and the banner appeared against a healthy
   server. All 798 drives now page through cleanly.

ALSO FIXED

- Position timestamps kept only whole seconds through the local cache, so a
  four-minute drive's 747 samples collapsed onto 211 instants. Charts silently
  dropped marks and drew a self-intersecting fill. Sub-second precision now
  survives the round trip.
- Dense sample charts are decimated to a few hundred marks, keeping each
  bucket's minimum and maximum so peaks are preserved rather than averaged away.
  Past a few hundred marks Swift Charts was truncating the trace partway across
  the plot.
- A failure fetching drives no longer costs the charge history: the two legs of
  a sync run independently, and a partial pass is not recorded as complete.

TRIMMED

Explanatory paragraphs under the charts are gone, along with the long Settings
and analytics captions. The axes and legends carry it. Period comparisons read
"+78% vs prev. 30d" instead of truncating mid-word.

KNOWN GAPS

Vehicle commands still go through TeslaMateApi; the backend holds no vehicle
credentials and forwards those actions.

App Store screenshots have not been re-shot and there is still no iPad set.
Both are needed before a store submission; neither blocks TestFlight.
