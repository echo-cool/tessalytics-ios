Tessalytics 1.8.5 — send a place to the car, and share any page as one picture.

WHAT TO TEST FIRST

1. SEND TO CAR (home screen, while the car is parked)

There is a new "Send to car" section under the direct controls. It lists places the
car has actually been — sorted by how often you go there, how recently, or how near
the car is standing now — with a filter for the rest and a cap of ten.

Tap one. The share sheet opens with a map link; pick Tesla and the destination lands
on the car's navigation. No Tesla account, no token, nothing to connect.

Please check:
- The places are ones you recognise, and "Most visited" really is your most visited.
- "Nearest" measures from where the car is now. With no reported position it falls
  back to most-visited rather than inventing an order.
- The Tesla app actually accepts it and the car navigates to the right place. This
  is the part I cannot verify from here.
- The section is absent while the car is driving. That is deliberate.

The link carries the coordinate, not the name. The car re-geocodes whatever it is
handed, and "Home" or "Whole Foods" would resolve to whichever one the car's own
search prefers rather than the one you have been parking at.

This cannot go through TeslaMate instead. TeslaMate is a logger — it reads the car
and writes rows, and neither it nor TeslaMateApi has any endpoint that sends a
command to a vehicle.

2. SHARE A PAGE

Eleven screens now have a share button in the top right: the home screen, battery
health, achievements, software updates, tyres, places, analysis, a drive, a charge,
drives and charging.

What it produces is a picture of the whole page, not a screenshot of whatever was
scrolled into view. The page is laid out again at a fixed width and drawn in one
piece however long it is, with the figures summarised at the top, a Tessalytics mark
in the bottom right, and a written summary travelling beside it so a message thread
quotes real numbers rather than just showing an image.

Please check:
- The share sheet previews the picture rather than the text.
- Maps appear in the image rather than as blank rectangles — the drive route, the
  places map, the car's location on the home screen.
- Nothing is in the picture that should not be: the analytics period picker, the
  Game Center sign-in card and the charging map are all left out on purpose,
  because a control is not a fact.
- A long page — a drive with five charts — comes out whole.

ALSO CHANGED

Direct Tesla is now developer-only. The Owner API is unofficial and undocumented,
and Tesla retires parts of it without notice: /api/1/vehicles has answered 412 since
January 2023. It is no longer offered in Settings or on the home screen unless debug
mode is unlocked — tap the version number in Settings five times. Nothing that reads
from TeslaMate is affected; if you had it connected, your token is untouched and the
screen is where it always was, behind the unlock.

If you rely on the direct controls day to day, say so — this is a judgement about
what an owner should be asked to depend on, not a removal.
