Tessalytics 1.9.0 — five more languages, and units you can choose.

LANGUAGES

The app now speaks English, Deutsch, Français, 日本語, 简体中文 and 繁體中文.
Settings → Language. The change lands immediately, no relaunch.

Simplified and Traditional Chinese have separate translations, because they differ
in vocabulary as well as script — 軟體 not 软件, 網路 not 网络.

These translations are mine, not a native speaker's. If a phrase reads oddly or is
plainly wrong, tell me which screen and what it should say — that is the most
useful thing you can send back from this build.

UNITS

Settings → Units now offers "From the car", "Metric" and "Imperial".

Values arrive from TeslaMate in whatever units your car is set to, so choosing
another one *converts* them — it does not just change the label. A car reporting
psi shows real bar, not the psi number with "bar" written under it. Your car's own
setting is not touched.

Please check the home card in particular: range, odometer and tyre pressures all
convert, and those were the three that had to be fixed by hand after the first
attempt showed 42.1 "bar".

WORDS THAT WERE STILL ENGLISH

About fifty phrases built their text at runtime — "131 mi · 7 days", "updated at
5:58 PM", "mi on the odometer" — so there was no fixed phrase to translate and they
stayed English on a translated screen. They are now proper format strings, which
also lets a language put the number and the words in its own order.

If you still find English on a translated screen, say where. Some of it is
deliberate — bare units like kWh and Wh/km, and your own place and vehicle names,
are left alone on purpose.

ALSO

German is longer than English, and the hero card's "mi estimated range" label was
being cut to "mi geschätzte Reichw…". It wraps to two lines now.
