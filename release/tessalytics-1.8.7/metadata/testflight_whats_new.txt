Tessalytics 1.8.7 — the charge forecast, corrected by a real charge.

1.8.6 added the forecast. Putting it in front of an actual car found three things
wrong with it, all visible in one screenshot of a wall box.

THE CHART NOW STARTS WHEN THE CHARGE DID

It started at "now". The app only sees readings from the moment it is opened, so a
car that had been plugged in for an hour showed a chart beginning a second ago,
labelled as though the charge had just started.

It now takes the start time from the car, and works back to the level the charge
began at from the energy the car says it has taken. That earlier stretch is drawn
as a fine dotted line, because it is inferred rather than watched — the app was not
there for it.

THE SLOWDOWN IS NOW WHERE IT ACTUALLY HAPPENS

The bigger error. A wall box holds its rate: 7 kW at 64% and 7 kW at 85%. The car
still reports a longer time to full than that flat rate implies, because it eases
off near 100% to balance the pack — and 1.8.6 took that difference and spread it
across the whole charge. The result was a forecast that put 80% about forty-five
minutes later than it actually arrives.

The taper is now applied where it belongs: flat until about 90%, then easing. A DC
charge is unchanged, because there the slowdown really has already begun — a pack
taking 100 %/h is at a current the chemistry is limiting from that moment on. The
app tells the two apart by the rate itself, not by anything it has to be told.

Please check the milestone times against what actually happens on a wall box. That
is the number this release exists to fix.

ALSO

- The right-hand axis was ticking at 2.5 kW and rounding the labels for display, so
  a 7 kW charge was drawn against an axis reading 0, 3, 5, 8, 10. Round numbers now.
- "now" and the plug-in time were printed on top of each other.
