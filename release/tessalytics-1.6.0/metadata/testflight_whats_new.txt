Tessalytics 1.6.0 — a rebuilt home screen, real driven paths, interactive charts,
and your car's actual rating.

WHAT TO TEST FIRST

1. Settings > Vehicle > Vehicle rating. Enter what your pack was rated at when
   new. The derived figure is the best your recorded history can support, and that
   is simply wrong when logging began after the pack had aged: an 84 kWh car reads
   as 74 if TeslaMate started at 15,000 miles. Health, capacity lost, range lost
   and equivalent cycles all inherited the error. Health updates live as you type
   — 84 kWh turns 97.4% into 86.3% — and every screen follows. Leave the fields
   blank to go back to the derived values.

2. The top card. It no longer announces "Offline at ..." — a car is asleep or
   offline almost all the time, and that was the loudest line in the app spent on
   the least surprising fact. Now it shows where the car is, and only motion or
   charging gets the prominent line. Battery is a ring rather than a number, the
   odometer sits beside the range, and the bottom band adds a seven-day bar strip,
   your typical consumption and battery health.

3. Places. The line is the route you actually drove. It used to join drive
   endpoints, which drew a straight chord through the hills between San Jose and
   Modesto. The server now aggregates 1.7 million position rows into a simplified
   path — one polyline per journey, so the gap between two trips is never drawn as
   travel — and the app caches it and only refetches when there is a new drive.

4. Tap any chart. Battery capacity and Recent driving open an explorer: touch and
   hold to put a pivot line on a point and read its exact value, switch between
   line, bar, area and pie, and read the whole series as a table with total,
   average, highest and lowest. The reading stays after you lift your finger,
   because a value you cannot look at is no use.

5. Battery capacity on the home screen. The five-figure text strip is gone; the
   capacity-by-mileage chart is there instead, and its mileage axis starts where
   your data starts rather than at zero.

ALSO CHANGED

- Only Tessalytics Backend is supported now. The TeslaMateApi client is gone
  along with the server-kind detection: one code path instead of two, and no more
  fixing everything twice.
- Elevation, outside temperature and pack voltage no longer anchor their axis to
  zero. Zero is not a meaningful floor for any of them, and anchoring flattened
  the variation into a line. Speed, power and energy still keep zero.
- Cards in a row line up again. A card with no detail line was letting its value
  drop lower than its neighbours.
- Vehicle commands moved above the analysis cards; a command is what you open the
  app to do.
