Tessalytics 1.7.0 — live driving mode, over server-sent events.

WHAT TO TEST FIRST

Mount the phone and drive. That is the case this build is for, and the case the
app was worst at: it used to show "Driving" and a speed that was up to thirty
seconds stale, and nothing else.

1. Readings now arrive as TeslaMate publishes them. The app opens an event stream
   to the backend instead of polling, so the speed on screen is the speed the car
   just reported. A LIVE badge pulses while the stream is connected and says
   RECONNECTING when it is not — a drive goes through tunnels, and you should
   never be looking at a stale number that claims to be current.

2. The hero changes while driving. A map follows the car, pointing the way it is
   facing, with the route just travelled drawn behind it. Speed, power, outside
   temperature and elevation replace the week-long battery chart, because the
   shape of the last seven days is not what you need at a glance from a mount.

3. New cards below it: distance, top speed, energy used net of regeneration,
   consumption, and peak power and regen — plus live speed and power charts over
   the last fifteen minutes. Power keeps zero on its axis and draws regeneration
   below it in green, because the sign is the interesting part.

4. The theme shifts. A stronger wash and a thicker accent bar say the screen is
   live. It is deliberately a shift within the same palette rather than a
   different look: the app should still look like itself, and a driver has no
   attention to spare for relearning a layout.

5. Tap Settings, then Explore demo data with the app relaunched — or simply drive.
   If you want to see live mode without waiting for a journey, there is a
   generated one behind the demo.

ALSO CHANGED

- The vehicle state is named again — Parked, Asleep, Offline — as a pill beside
  the place rather than the headline it was two releases ago.
- Servers can be renamed and removed. Tap one in Settings. Renaming does not
  re-verify or re-sync, because fixing a typo in a label should not cost 800
  drives.
- Charging sessions draw level against power on one chart, with the level climbing
  and the power tapering on their own axes. Each row in the charging list carries
  the same curve as a thumbnail.
- Voltage and current are only plotted while the charger is delivering. Both
  collapse when it stops, which drew a cliff where the pack voltage actually rises
  375 to 402 V as it fills. TeslaMate's AC current sensor reads nothing during a
  DC fast charge, so that chart is now absent rather than a flat zero line.
- A sleeping car shows its last known state with the time it was taken, instead of
  six tiles of "Unknown". Remembered readings are muted, because the green of a
  live "Locked" would claim more than the app knows.

KNOWN GAPS

The stream needs Tessalytics Backend at a version with `/v1/vehicles/{id}/stream`.
Older deployments fall back to polling with no loss of function.

Battery use is higher in live mode than parked, by design: it holds a connection
open and redraws on every reading. The stream closes when the app goes to the
background.
