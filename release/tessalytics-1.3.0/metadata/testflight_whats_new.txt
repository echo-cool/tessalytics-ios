Tessalytics 1.3.0 — iPad support, and a dashboard that stops guessing.

WHAT TO TEST FIRST

1. iPad. The app now runs natively on iPad in every orientation, with a
   collapsible sidebar in place of the tab bar and cards held to a readable
   width instead of stretched edge to edge. Try Split View and Stage Manager.

2. A sleeping car. Park the car and let it fall asleep, then open Status.
   TeslaMateApi cannot read live telemetry while a car is asleep, and it
   reports missing values as zeros — which the previous build displayed as
   fact. It said "Unlocked" for a locked car and "0 mi estimated range" for a
   full battery. Now the range falls back to the rated figure and says so, lock
   and opening state read "Unknown", and the top card switches to synchronized
   history: last drive, distance over seven days, last charge. Please confirm
   nothing on that screen claims something it cannot know.

3. First run after adding a server. Configure a TeslaMate server from scratch.
   The dashboard used to sit on "Status not available yet" until you changed
   vehicle or backgrounded the app; it should now populate on its own.

4. Pull to refresh, on Status and on both Activity tabs. The spinner used to be
   able to stick open. It should now finish exactly when the data lands.

5. The Activity drive list. Each row used to host a live map, which made
   scrolling stutter. Routes are now cached static snapshots, and larger.

ALSO CHANGED

- Home screen cards are tappable and lead to the matching detail screen; new
  shortcut row for Drives, Charging, Analysis and Battery; a "Latest drive"
  card with its route.
- Charging history leads with energy added and average power, and shows a price
  only where one is actually configured, instead of a column of $0.00.
- Every chart names both axes with units, labels its values and carries a
  legend. Drive and charge charts plot against time rather than a database row
  id, so the shape of a trip is readable.
- Metric cards are more compact and every card in a row is the same height.
- Back is top-left everywhere, and the edge-swipe back gesture works on the
  detail screens where a custom back button had disabled it.

KNOWN GAPS
