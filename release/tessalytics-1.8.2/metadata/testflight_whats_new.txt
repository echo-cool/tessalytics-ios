Tessalytics 1.8.2 — the address on the home screen is where the car actually is.

WHAT TO TEST FIRST

Drive somewhere. The address under "Driving" used to say your home address for
the whole journey.

The app was reading TeslaMate's geofence, and TeslaMate only names a place when a
*drive* ended inside a geofence you had drawn — so the answer was the last named
place the car had visited, not where it was. Correct data, answering a different
question, which is the worst kind of wrong because it looks right.

The geofence is no longer used for this at all. The address is reverse-geocoded on
this device with MapKit from the coordinate your server reports: the road you are
on while moving, the full address once stopped. Throttled, so a drive costs a
handful of lookups rather than one per reading.

THEN PARK IT

A parked Tesla goes to sleep and stops reporting a position entirely. The card now
falls back to the last position reported while the car was awake — it has not
moved since, so that is still where it is. This is the same rule the tyre
pressures already followed.

If nothing resolves at all — no position ever recorded, or no network — the line
is hidden rather than guessed at.

The Location row under "Software & details" follows the same rule.

EVERYTHING FROM 1.8.1 IS STILL HERE

- Direct Tesla works again (the HTTP 412), and asks only for a refresh token.
- The hero card's figures each lead somewhere: battery ring and range to battery
  health, odometer and the week's driving to drives, the tyres to a tyre screen,
  the car's name to vehicle settings with the rating.
- Software updates as a timeline, with days spent on each version.
- Achievements in Settings, reported to Game Center when you are signed in.
- Range to two decimals, odometer to one, and the gear the car is in.
