Tessalytics 1.9.1 — three layout fixes at large text sizes.

Found by setting an iPhone to an accessibility text size and reading the German
and Japanese screens. All three only appear at those sizes; nothing changes at the
default.

- A parked car's address collapsed to "1350…". It was sharing a row with the gear
  and state badges under a one-line limit. It now gets the width of the card to
  itself and wraps, because where the car is standing is the whole point of that
  line.

- The odometer icon drew on top of its own label. The icon's box was a fixed size
  while the symbol inside it grew with the text, so it spilled over the words
  beside it. The two icons beside the battery ring now step aside at accessibility
  sizes; the labels say what the figures are without them.

- "Auswertung" was cut to "Auswer…" in the four buttons under the vehicle card.
  Two lines now.

Japanese was clean throughout — it sets taller rather than wider, so nothing ran
out of room.

WHAT TO TEST

Settings → Accessibility → Display & Text Size → Larger Text, pushed well up, then
the home screen and Settings in German, Japanese and your own language. Anything
cut off, overlapping or unreachable is worth reporting — especially in a language I
have not looked at at that size.
