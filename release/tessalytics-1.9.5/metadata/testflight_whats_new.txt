Tessalytics 1.9.5 — the shared image gets its map back, and the home chart gains
a second axis.

THE MAP IN THE SHARED IMAGE

1.9.4 made the share button quick by refusing to wait more than a second and a
half for map tiles. On a slower connection that turned out to mean the poster came
out with a grey "route not available" panel where the map belongs. Quick, and
wrong.

That was one number doing two jobs, so it is now two. When there is no map to
fall back on, a share waits — up to five seconds, because a poster missing its
picture is a worse outcome than a slow one. When a usable map is already in hand,
it waits four tenths of a second, uses what it has, and refreshes behind.

More to the point, the maps are now fetched while you are simply reading the home
screen, so by the time you tap share they are already here. That is what makes
the share both immediate and complete, which no single timeout could manage.

The cost, stated plainly: the app now fetches one small map snapshot per distinct
location while the home screen is open, whether or not you ever share it. It is
skipped while the car is driving. If you would rather it did not, say so — the
share works correctly without it, just more slowly the first time.

THE HOME SCREEN CHART HAS TWO AXES

The seven-day battery trace now carries the odometer over the same week: battery
percentage on the left in green, odometer on the right in grey, dashed. The axis
labels are coloured to match their lines, so which side belongs to which is
readable without a legend.

The odometer reading is taken from the ends of each drive and from charging
sessions, because that is the only place TeslaMate records it. A reading that
would take the line backwards is discarded — an odometer only climbs.

WHAT TO TEST

Share the home screen. First, straight after opening the app; then again after
sitting on the screen for a few seconds. Both should give you a poster with the
map in it, and the second should be instant.

The case I most want to hear about is the first one on a poor connection. If a
poster still comes out with a grey panel where the map should be, that is the bug
from 1.9.4 not fully fixed, and I would like to know.

Then the chart at the top of the home screen. The odometer line should climb left
to right and never dip. Check the right-hand numbers look like your actual
odometer over the last week — if they are wrong, the two scales are out of step
and the chart is lying, which matters more than it looks.

Sharing a drive, the places map and the analysis screens all use the same
machinery, so they are worth a look too.
