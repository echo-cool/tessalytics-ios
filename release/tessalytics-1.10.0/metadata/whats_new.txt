Now on iPad and Mac.

Tessalytics runs on iPad, and on Apple Silicon Macs as the iPad app. On a wide
screen the tab bar becomes a sidebar and the cards stop stretching.

Your servers follow your Apple Account. Add a server on your iPhone and your iPad
already has it — server settings sync through iCloud, and credentials through
iCloud Keychain, which is end-to-end encrypted. Cached driving history stays on
each device and is re-read from your own server.

A proper first run. Connecting a server with a long history used to drop you on a
dashboard that said there was nothing to show while it was still downloading.
Now it shows what it is fetching, and counts your drives and charges as they
arrive.

Two new analysis views. Efficiency plots consumption against outside temperature
and trip length; Mileage covers the odometer, distance and charging by month, and
where your time actually goes. The home screen chart now carries the odometer
beside the battery over the same week.

Also: the shared image keeps its map on a slow connection, the vehicle rating
picker accepts "Not set", and there is a good deal less text throughout.
