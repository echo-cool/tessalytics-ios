Understand every drive.

Tessalytics is a private, native companion for your self-hosted TeslaMate installation, on iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Connect it to your own Tessalytics Backend to monitor vehicle status, explore driving and charging history, and turn your data into useful insights.

REQUIRES A SELF-HOSTED SERVER
Tessalytics needs an existing TeslaMate installation plus Tessalytics Backend, a read-only API service you deploy beside it — the same Docker Compose file is fine. The address you enter in the app is the backend's, not TeslaMate's or Grafana's. This is not a standalone vehicle account or a hosted service.

STATUS AT A GLANCE
• Battery level and estimated range
• Charging state, power, progress, and time remaining
• Location, climate, lock, doors, windows, trunks, and tire pressure
• Honest live, stale, asleep, offline, and unavailable labels

HISTORY THAT WORKS OFFLINE
• Incrementally synchronized drives and charging sessions
• Route maps plus speed, power, elevation, and temperature charts
• Charging energy, efficiency, cost, voltage, and current
• Multiple servers and multiple vehicles

ANALYSIS BEYOND A DASHBOARD
• Distance, driving time, energy, cost, efficiency, and destination trends
• Consumption against outside temperature and trip length
• Odometer, distance and charging by month, and where your time actually goes
• Battery capacity, range, health, and standby-drain estimates
• On-device travel, charging-time, cost, and efficiency forecasts
• Optional local notifications for low battery, charge completion, software updates, and important changes

BUILT FOR EVERY SCREEN
On iPad and Mac the dashboard becomes a two- or three-column board you can rearrange, and it remembers where you put each card.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your device connects directly to the server you configure. Tessalytics has no developer-operated cloud, advertising, tracking, or analytics SDK. Server settings follow your Apple Account through iCloud, and credentials through iCloud Keychain, which is end-to-end encrypted — turn iCloud Keychain off and they stay on one device. Synchronized history is never uploaded; it stays on the device that fetched it.

OPTIONAL DIRECT CONNECTION
Advanced users can add an Owner API access/refresh-token pair generated outside Tessalytics for direct live state and a small set of controls. The app never asks for your Tesla password. Every command requires confirmation and Face ID or the device passcode. This unofficial API is optional and may change.

Tessalytics is an unofficial community tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or supported by the official TeslaMate project or Tesla, Inc. Battery health and predictions are estimates, not diagnostics, guarantees, or safety guidance.
