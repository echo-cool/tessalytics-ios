Tessalytics 1.10.0 — runs on iPad and Mac, follows your Apple Account, and no
longer tells you to "drive more" while it downloads your drives.

FIRST RUN WAS TELLING YOU SOMETHING FALSE

If you connected a server with a real history behind it, the app went straight to
the dashboard and started fetching underneath it. Every card showed its own
version of "not enough data yet — drive more and check back", while those very
drives were downloading. The app was describing an empty database instead of a
busy one, and the more history you had, the more wrong it was.

There is now a screen that shows what it is doing: finding your vehicles, reading
the status, downloading drives and charges with a live count as pages land, then
modelling battery health. No percentage bar — the API reports no total, and a bar
with an invented denominator would be a second untruth. "Continue without
waiting" appears after six seconds if you would rather not.

IPAD AND MAC

The app now runs on iPad properly and on Apple Silicon Macs as the iPad app. The
tab bar becomes a sidebar on a wide screen, and the cards stop stretching.

YOUR SERVERS AND TOKENS FOLLOW YOUR APPLE ACCOUNT

Add a server on your iPhone and your iPad already has it. Server profiles travel
through iCloud key-value storage; credentials and Owner API tokens travel through
iCloud Keychain, which is end-to-end encrypted — Apple cannot read it and neither
can anyone but your own signed-in devices.

Stated plainly, because it is a change and not only a feature: before this
release those tokens were marked "this device only" and could not leave the
device at all. That is the guarantee being traded for not having to set up each
device by hand. If you would rather keep it, turn off iCloud Keychain in iOS
Settings and the items stay local. Cached history does not sync — it stays on the
device that fetched it, and is re-fetched from your own server elsewhere.

WHAT TO TEST

The first-run screen is the one I most want eyes on, and it needs a real server
with real history. Connect one and watch: do the counts climb, does it land on a
dashboard with your data already in it, and does the whole thing feel like
progress rather than a hang? If you have a large history, roughly how long?

Then sign in on a second device with the same Apple Account. The server should
already be listed, and it should connect without you pasting a token again. If it
does not, tell me — iCloud Keychain propagation is not instant, and I would like
to know how long it actually took for you.

On iPad and on a Mac: anything stretched, clipped, or unreachable. The app has
been checked on an iPad Pro 13 and looks right there, which is not the same as
every size being right.
