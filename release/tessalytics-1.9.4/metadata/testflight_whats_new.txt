Tessalytics 1.9.4 — the share button no longer makes you wait.

One fix. If you tapped share on the home screen and sat watching the spinner,
this is why.

The poster carries a map, and the map is a round trip to Apple's tile servers.
Timed on the home screen: fetching the tiles took 0.9 seconds, drawing the whole
poster took 0.05, and encoding the PNG took 0.04. So the drawing was never the
problem — the wait was the map, and two things made it much worse than 0.9
seconds.

The wait had no ceiling from your side. The fetch gave up after six seconds, but
the share sheet waited out every one of them, so a weak signal meant six seconds
of spinner with nothing to show for it.

And the cached map was almost never reused. It was matched on exact coordinates,
and a parked car's reported position drifts by centimetres between readings — so
a map that had not moved was fetched again on every single share.

Now the wait is capped at a second and a half. If the tiles arrive in that time
they go in the poster as before. If they do not, you get your share sheet
anyway, and the fetch keeps running in the background — so the next share has
the map and is instant. Maps are matched to within about a metre, so a parked car
reuses the one it already has.

WHAT TO TEST

The share button, top right of the home screen. It should open the sheet
promptly. Try it on wifi, on cellular, and somewhere with a poor signal.

Then share the same screen twice in a row: the second one should be immediate.

The thing I would most like to know: on a first share, on your connection, does
the poster have the map in it? If it is missing more often than not, the cap is
set too low for real conditions and I would rather raise it than have you
sharing mapless posters.

Also worth a look — the same fix covers the share buttons on a drive, on the
places map, and on the analysis screens. A poster that comes out with a grey
"route not available" panel where a map should be is worth reporting, especially
if a second attempt fixes it.
