Tessalytics 1.8.1 — Direct Tesla works again, and the hero card leads somewhere.

WHAT TO TEST FIRST

1. Direct Tesla. It failed at the first request with HTTP 412 because Tesla made
   /api/1/vehicles answer that in January 2023 and never brought it back. The
   account's cars now come from /api/1/products, which still works.

   And it only asks for the REFRESH token now. Paste that one field and connect;
   Tessalytics exchanges it for an access token itself. If you have a Powerwall
   or solar, they should not appear in the vehicle picker.

2. Tap things on the hero card. Each figure now leads where the figure is about:
   - battery ring or range → battery health
   - odometer or the week's driving → drives
   - the tyre diagram → a tyre screen with each corner and the car's own warnings
   - the car's name → vehicle settings, which holds the rating when new
   All of them used to open battery health, including the tyres.

3. Software updates. A timeline bar per version across the months it ran, and
   each row says how many days the car spent on that version.

4. Achievements, in Settings. Twelve of them, computed from your synced history —
   distance, a drive in one sitting, energy through the pack, consecutive days,
   places visited, pack health held past 50,000 km. They are listed whether or
   not you are signed in to Game Center; when you are, progress is recorded there.

   Note: the achievements will not appear in Game Center itself until they are
   created in App Store Connect. The in-app list works regardless.

ALSO IN THIS BUILD

- Estimated range shows two decimals and the odometer shows one. Rounded to whole
  units, a range that was visibly falling looked like one that was stuck.
- The gear the car reports — P, D, R, N — is shown beside the state. The app was
  reading it on every reading and showing it nowhere.
