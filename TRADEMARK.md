# Tessalytics Trademark Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

## 1. Purpose

This policy exists to protect the integrity of the **Tessalytics** projects and
the people who use them. It ensures that a user can tell the difference between
this open-source project and an unofficial, commercial, or deceptive third-party
offering.

It exists alongside the software's [licence](LICENSE) (AGPLv3). **The AGPLv3
covers the code; this policy covers the branding.** They answer different
questions, and neither one substitutes for the other.

This policy is modelled on, and deliberately compatible with, the
[TeslaMate Trademark Policy](https://github.com/teslamate-org/teslamate/blob/main/TRADEMARK.md).

## 2. Protected Marks

The marks are the name **Tessalytics** (the "brand"), the three projects it names
— the iPhone app, the backend, and the web dashboard — and the related visual
identity, including the application icon (the "Logos"). The name is always
written as a single capitalised word: Tessalytics (never "TessAlytics",
"tessalytics" in prose, "Tesla-lytics", or any other variation).

## 3. Philosophy

Tessalytics is open source and exists to be self-hosted. Your car's history is
yours, and it should stay on hardware you control.

Individuals, communities and non-profits are free to use the Tessalytics name and
Logos for non-commercial purposes. Commercial use of the brand is reserved.

## 4. Permitted Use (No Permission Required)

You may use the name "Tessalytics" and the Logos without asking, for:

- **Personal self-hosting** — running any of these projects for yourself, your
  friends, or your family, including on hardware you rent.
- **Compatibility notices** — describing your own tool's technical compatibility
  (e.g. "works with Tessalytics Backend").
- **Non-commercial writing and teaching** — blogs, tutorials, videos, forum posts
  and conference talks about the project.
- **Open-source additions** — free, AGPL-compatible plugins, forks, packages and
  container images may use the name to state what they interoperate with, provided
  they do not imply they are official.
- **Nominative references** — talking about, writing about, comparing, or
  demonstrating Tessalytics truthfully.

## 5. Prohibited Use (Without Prior Written Consent)

- **Product names** — using "Tessalytics" in the name of any commercial or
  closed-source product, application or service, including constructions like
  "[Product] for Tessalytics" or "Tessalytics Pro".
- **Misleading affiliation** — any name, branding or presentation that suggests an
  official endorsement or partnership, or that your build is the official,
  "supported", or "premium" version.
- **Company and brand names** — using the name in a company name, brand, or
  product line.
- **Paid services under the name** — marketing commercial hosting, support,
  consulting or training as "Tessalytics" hosting, support, consulting or
  training.
- **Domains and handles** — registering or using domains or social-media handles
  containing "Tessalytics" that imply official status.
- **Merchandise** — producing goods bearing the name or Logos for sale.
- **Deceptive use** — using the name or Logos to distribute malware, to track
  people without their consent, for anything illegal, or to sell what is
  presented as the official software.

Running a modified version of this software as a network service is governed by
the AGPLv3, which requires you to offer your users the corresponding source. Doing
so under a different name is permitted; doing so under this one is not.

## 6. Requirements for Third-Party Projects

Any unofficial tool that interfaces with Tessalytics — through its API, its
database, or its pairing flow — must carry a prominent disclaimer in its README
and/or About screen:

> "This project is an unofficial community tool and is not affiliated with,
> endorsed by, or supported by the Tessalytics project."

## 7. Other People's Marks

Tessalytics is itself an unofficial community project. "TeslaMate" is the
trademark of the TeslaMate project and "Tesla" is the trademark of Tesla, Inc.;
both are used here only nominatively, to say what this software connects to, in
keeping with TeslaMate's own trademark policy. Nothing in this document claims any
right in either mark, and nothing here should be read as a claim of affiliation
with or endorsement by either.

## 8. Enforcement and Changes

The project reserves the right to act on infringement, including trademark
complaints and takedown notices, against anyone violating this policy.

This policy may be updated from time to time; changes are recorded in this file
and in the repository's history.
